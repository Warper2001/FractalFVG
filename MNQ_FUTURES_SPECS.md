# MNQ Futures Specifications

## Contract Details
- **Underlying**: Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 Index
- **Ticker**: MNQ
- **Exchange**: CME (Chicago Mercantile Exchange)
- **Contract Size**: $2 x Nasdaq-100 Index
- **Tick Size**: 0.25 index points = $0.50 per tick
- **Point Value**: $2.00 per index point

## Margin & Leverage
- **Initial Margin**: ~$2,200 (varies by volatility)
- **Maintenance Margin**: ~$2,000 (varies by volatility)
- **Leverage**: Approximately 20:1 (based on contract value)
- **Contract Value**: Nasdaq-100 Index × $2 (e.g., 17,500 × $2 = $35,000)

## Commission Structure
- **Commission**: $0.50 per side ($1.00 round turn)
- **Exchange Fees**: ~$0.20 per side
- **NFA Fees**: ~$0.02 per side
- **Total Cost**: ~$1.44 round turn per contract

## Risk Management
- **Max Drawdown**: Should be measured in dollars, not percentage
- **Position Sizing**: Based on margin requirements, not cash
- **Risk per Trade**: Typically 1-2% of account equity
- **Stop Loss**: Usually 5-10 ticks ($2.50-$5.00)

## Example Calculations
- **Contract Value**: 17,500 × $2 = $35,000
- **Margin Required**: ~$2,200
- **Leverage**: $35,000 ÷ $2,200 ≈ 16x leverage
- **Commission**: $0.50 entry + $0.50 exit = $1.00 total
- **Tick Value**: 0.25 points = $0.50
- **5-tick stop**: 5 × $0.50 = $2.50 risk per contract
